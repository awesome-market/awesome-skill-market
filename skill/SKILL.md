# Deslop

Rules distilled from upstream design skills by the deslop-fusion pipeline. **Do not edit this file** — change `decisions/` or upstream and rebuild.

## The Floor — never ship a violation

- Prefer the client's own photographs, real captures, built products, or live software UI over library stock, 3D renders, AI composites, mood imagery, faces, or drawn stand-ins; only use renders or library photos when the brief explicitly requests them or the subject is unreachable.
- Only place a real photograph where the subject genuinely exists; never substitute renders, drawings, effects, empty placeholders, stock people, or another maker's product for the client's actual product, place, interface, or work, and never present one object kind as another.
- Keep every word as live text sized to fit rather than baking copy into an image, and delete decoration that carries no fact such as rotated labels, scroll cues, version pills, weather strips, mono-caps strips, stock credits, diagram captions, and text on place photographs.
- Invent no fact, figure, name, price, hour, detail, or spec the brief or client files supply; leave the section empty or shrink it when a field is missing, and never present a fabricated series.
- Keep imagery sparse and appropriate to the page: at most one diagram per long answer, one face photograph in print, distinct per-section photos, at most four slots per row, at most six real customer logos per software/product page, library photos capped at one third of total pictures unless lifted, whole products shown in at most three sections with different pictures each, and photographs limited to at most a third of the page below the hero with no two photograph-led sections in a row.
- Use one or two eyebrow labels per page and none when the heading already names the thing, read the copy aloud as the client and cut sentences they would not say about themselves, and keep the footer factual with matching hours and no sign-off jokes.
- Every media slot must hold a real asset with a valid src, explicit dimensions, and a named comment when it is a stand-in; never leave slots empty, greyed, blacked, washed, gradient-filled, or populated by drawn illustrations, fake UI, stat strips, or logo walls.
- Never display the same photograph twice on a page, including smaller files, crops, or mirrors of an existing frame, nor three near-identical frames; never hotlink unrelated sites, redraw or mock real marks, use stock/AI/composites, or draw look-alikes.
- Keep the footer to facts—legal row, brand, year, Privacy, Terms, credits, street, district, and place hours—without a sign-off line, email sign-up, or render credits, and never name people under photographs.
- Keep the hero to seven words or fewer in the headline plus one lead sentence and at most two actions, make section headings noun phrases or short plain claims of one to six words in at most two lines, and make buttons a verb plus what the visitor gets in one to five words.
- Write every line as a concrete, specific fact in the interface's own voice using the owner's nouns and never a line a competitor could reuse unchanged, lead with the true difference and its proof instead of unproven adjectives or slogans, prefer verbs over adjectives and avoid adjective triads and value pillars, avoid exclamation marks in body copy and number-pair headings, and never tack an -ing phrase onto a fact.
- Give forms without known endpoints an empty data-endpoint, state nothing was sent, never print a success state, and never drive an open-now line from assumed hours; paste the single credit line in the footer with the full credits list on its own page; keep .socials empty unless the brief names accounts; admit exactly one limit per page from the brief or plain trade facts; get directions requires a street, 'See the area' requires a district, and no map link may use a town only; and write field hints only for what the brief promises.
- Size inline images to the body measure rather than full-bleed or exceeding 2.2x shown width, set width/height/aspect-ratio on every image, render below-fold photos as img elements with srcset and picture for crops, lazy-load everything below the fold except the LCP hero, and keep total page weight under 2400 KiB at 1440px width.
- Give every image a working source with explicit width/height or aspect-ratio, lazy-load below-fold images with decoding=async while keeping the hero eager via fetchpriority=high, serve AVIF or WebP through srcset sized to layout width, and never show visible placeholder labels, ratio notes, sample chips, device chrome, or drawn browser bars.
- Keep product/service/place grounds as flat CSS colours with no gradients, textures, glass, filters, or shadows on the interface; never use grain, noise, coloured glow, gradient text, glass, frosted effects, or blend modes beyond effect-internal jobs unless the theme earns a budgeted recipe; reserve dark pages/heroes and shader grounds for tech pages only.
- Never leave residue artifacts or fabricated claims, metrics, testimonials, figures, superlatives, invented details, or coined names on the rendered page; state only what the brief provides and represent missing facts as muted dashed slots, at most two per page.
- Never invent names, roles, companies, quotes, testimonials, stock phrases, reviews, ratings, counts, statistics, portraits, awards, press lines, products, logos, prices, dates, figures, or client photographs; carry over every existing fact exactly and show founders only using the names the brief gives.
- Prefer real captures of reachable products over built mocks, renders, or drawings, and never place a photograph where the real interface, product render, or type belongs.
- Do not apply CSS invert/brightness to raster images in dark mode, avoid duotone or tint on pale low-tonal-range subjects, keep at most one texture artifact family per page, and never place effects over or in place of the client's photographs.
- Only assign a raster medium when the region has a real source asset, library photograph, generated still, built product, or large-set number; never paint the subject with paper, grain, stamps, dials, knobs, bevels, engravings, or hand-drawn marks, and never render objects, plants, animals, people, products, buildings, landscapes, or scenes past icon size as SVG or CSS.
- Ship stills by default with at most one live context per page, route non-moving effects through still() or fill(...,{still:true}) to draw once and free their context, pause off-screen and hidden-tab effects, drop pixel ratio when frames exceed 21-24ms, and under reduced motion render dither objects as a single still line drawing without WebGL or pause controls.
- Add a static dither to every dark gradient and use linear gradients only as an edge fade inside a visual never as decoration.
- Maintain 4.5:1 contrast for body text and APCA Lc 60, plus 3:1 or Lc 45 for large text, icons, focus rings, and field edges; derive focus tokens from the accent with at least 3:1 contrast against both paper levels and indicate disabled state through both colour and another visual cue.
- Build paper, ink, five to nine neutrals, one accent, and a focus colour on the accent hue; derive every dark-mode token from its light value by recipe rather than invert() or flipping greys, honour pinned brief directions exactly, and deal palettes by trade taking the first unless it clashes with client colours.
- Choose a writing register per client from copy-registers.md rather than applying one uniform house voice or the model's default register, and keep every sentence as live text.
- Build section shapes from the reader's questions with one idea and one action besides the main action, vary card length with what the brief knows, treat empty states as invitations to act with a fix or next step, and end placeholders with an example pattern.
- Provide prefers-reduced-motion fallbacks (single still frame, no hover/pause/slow turns, context freed) for every animation and transform including transition:all, keep content visible with scripting disabled, and ensure everything is readable at rest without script and under reduced motion.
- Give fx-photo an empty effects list and ignore data-cx-effect and disable its zoom under reduced motion, for kind=other load no animation library and no WebGL, serve scene photographs from the site itself since a host that sends no CORS headers cannot be printed, ensure the [data-mode] attribute wins over the media query by only applying media overrides when no explicit mode is set, name each feature-card landscape scene via data-cx-scene and composition via data-cx-params so no two panels look identical using day, dusk, meadow, or grain-gradient, and show success only after a real endpoint response and never write thank-you messages on submit or use a modal.
- Name the product category in the h1, plus the buyer or place for unknown brands, in two to eight words, and keep the hero h1 to two to nine words in a single clause.
- Name things by what users understand and state product facts as values with units, placing every number beside the claim it proves and keeping figures uneven where real.
- Write tab, sticky bar, nav, section heading, and error labels as concrete terms with a verb plus object naming the field and what to type, and write button verbs as the action.
- Price and state every perk literally with the number on the button, omit phone numbers and Call buttons unless the brief gives a number, use at most one brand line and one pun per page never in the h1, and print only a street and district.
- Never repeat a fact, stat, spec, figure, or quote—each appears once with one wording, place each number in the sentence it proves, repeat the product's own name instead of cycling through generic terms, and never name people under photographs.
- Limit each page to exactly one filled-primary button (one recipe per page, treated as its own page), route quiet variants elsewhere, and never place two filled buttons side by side.
- Keep exactly one main action per screen, show the CTA button in the sticky pane only after the section's last capture is reached, and never repeat Get directions beyond the hero button and phone bar.
- Route every colour, font-family, and surface value through tokens declared at :root or a theme block; never emit pure absolute colours (e.g. #000, #fff, oklch(0%/100%)) outside the token block, and never resolve a surface token to a pure absolute unless the stamp declares surface:absolute.
- Under the default Restrained posture, reserve accent colour for signals (focus rings, small tags, one control) and keep it under roughly 5% of viewport area (8% in the render tier).
- On dark grounds, force grain specks to white via the colour matrix, derive marks by lowering chroma and raising lightness with dark rule-color gridlines and halved fade-fill opacity, keep headings in --color-ink at 92-96% lightness with anchor-tinted chroma, and ensure all text and interactive elements meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Place exactly one typographic verb-led link on the plate under the sentence as the visible action, keep action names identical across the entire flow so the button label matches the resulting status or toast, and make the hero primary action a verb and object matching the nav, close, and phone bar word for word.
- Use real photographs of the subject set large and prominent, building interstitial sections from theme material rather than walls of small images, and ensure each picture occupies at least half a tile, a 50/50 split, or a whole screen layer.
- Keep the back third of the page calm before the close, place each moment in a different section with no two moments that avoid each other, drop the place section on pages over six screens but never the booking, use close-photo with an unused 1:1 photograph and no words on it, keep one height per card row and tile kind across the page with sections at H or 2H, disable scroll-snap scroll-jacking and smooth-scroll libraries keeping everything except big scenes within their own 1H or 2H sections, use a node-and-line diagram only to show how things connect and never to depict a screen or UI fragment, and make text containers handle long content with truncate line-clamp or break-words.
- Omit reveal animations: present content statically, keep targets visible at their end state when scripting is off or under reduced motion, and never hide or gate text the user may be reading.
- Never ship with an open FAIL or REFLEX; fix or waive reflexes, read finish findings, run checks in headless Chrome with the right environment, and finish only when the manual gate prints GATE: PASS.
- A still must render once, keep an img, free its context, and remove its canvases, do not use the dropped brands set, brands: ids must fail with an explanatory message, run the icon resolver as the final step never relying on CDNs, runtime tinting, or Simple Icons calls at runtime, and fail when a loaded assets file differs from the skill's copy beyond whitespace, quotes, semicolons, and commas.
- Ship zero broken requests and zero sideways overflow while keeping total requests under 110, sampling at one WebGL sample per CSS pixel or less with pixel ratio at most 1.5, keeping median long tasks under 20 and blocking time under 4 seconds with WebGL off, pausing every effect off-screen and in hidden tabs with a pause button for independent loops over about 5 seconds, and keeping stat with window:true so the ground shows through its bars.
- Climb the surface ladder via lightness steps and subtle borders with an 8px corner radius instead of hairlines, gradients, or material substitutes; make elevation tokens progressively lighter than paper in dark mode (+3% per level), disable drop shadows in dark mode expressing elevation through lightness or an inset hairline ring, and keep hover/press states to exactly one visual signal such as a colour step or 1px lift/drop.
- Prohibit glass panels or cards, gradient-filled pills, gradient text, and glass effects.
- Never state a fact a visitor could take as true—such as a price, date, name, count, response time, or place—unless the brief supplies it, and mark every assumed value with its own data-assumed element.
- Use one icon library per page with a single stroke width and style sized on the grid and colored currentColor, one aspect ratio per goods grid with matching backdrop, angle, and light, and never mix icon styles or reuse a stand-in photograph across sections.
- Display only the user's own logos, keeping each mark's proportions and colors intact, using one treatment per row at 28-48px tall, preferring full-color marks and falling back to single-color Simple Icons only where no color mark exists, and using file-type icons for document cards rather than brand marks.
- Exclude drop caps, fleurons, ornaments, transparent glass, browser and device chrome, recognizable viewpoints, named bar rooms, dithering, halftoning, grading, duotones, grain, glitch effects, and canvas overlays covering more than half of a photograph, reserving such effects for code-drawn objects only.
- Show proof only as small exact objects from the brief, picture every headline promise with a real image or cut the promise, and key stand-in slots must carry a named comment referencing the actual asset path.
- Use full-bleed stills, whole products on stages, inset frames, or statements followed by work in heroes without sliders, crossfades, gates, loaders, or entrance animations hiding words; keep hero photographs under 400 KiB and every other photograph under 250 KiB; allow picture-less sections only for price lists, FAQs, specs tables, booking forms, or single statements, never as neighbours.
- Do not use the manifesto structure for checkout, pricing, or product tour pages, and never use the Type Specimen pattern with an off-the-shelf typeface.
- Give each page at most one big scene, keep photos inside the kit gallery, and record the chosen scene and its section in the plan.
- Edit script templates rather than generated HTML, and never delete or edit the kit's own files to clear a check; a check that flags a kit file must be reported and the page rechecked after any change.
- Every field must have a visible label above it, never using placeholder as the label; state changes must never alter border width, padding, height, or animate the ring, reserving a transparent outline at rest so the ring appears without shifting; show the inset outline immediately on focus and never change the border width, keeping floating labels as placeholders at rest; textareas must allow vertical resize with min-height 6rem; range thumbs must take focus and provide a 44px hit target; keep list text at 16px and use 13px only for group labels; use logical properties for spacing, allow room for 30 to 40 percent longer strings, and avoid hard-coded date or number formats.
- Make all controls keyboard-operable with correct semantics: use button for actions and a/Link for navigation, implement custom widgets with ARIA roles/states/properties, and group compound controls with focus-within so focus rings cover the whole control.
- Give every interactive element a visible :focus-visible ring (at least 3:1 contrast, 2px action colour placed 2px off the control with no transition) and never remove the outline without providing an equivalent visible focus indicator.
- Give every control an accessible name (label or aria-label for icon-only buttons), every image alt text (empty for decorative), every decorative SVG/canvas/CSS-art aria-hidden=true, and ensure meaningful media provides captions, transcripts, or descriptions.
- Limit grain to a single non-animated tile made at load, never place it over UI, text, or photographs, use client photographs or renders for texture, logos, transparent glass, draping cloth, freeform organic shells, and anything thinner than a texel, and use still low-contrast grain-gradient inside chips, pills, and bars without silk, glass, or metal sheen.
- Never derive brand colours or fonts from memory, never ship a competitor-adjacent palette, never recolour/redraw/tint third-party logos, and never use violet or lavender anywhere in the page's own design including accents, grounds, gradients, effect colours, or tints.
- Keep the default palette soft and low-chroma with ground chroma under 0.12, tints tuned toward the accent rather than violet, gradients capped at three stops, and no purple-blue, purple-pink, cyan-on-dark, glow shadows, chromatic halos, spotlights, or alpha-defined colours.
- Place no words, captions, or descriptions on any photograph or visual, put licence credits in a single footer line, and never show UI fragments, dashboards, or widgets on gaming pages.
- Never ship a row of big stat numbers, filler stat strips, check-icon lists, or a customer-logo wall; instead lead with real photographs, built products, or large-set numbers.
- Choose exactly one image treatment from full-bleed, tightly cropped to the grid, inline with text, margin-aligned, or none, use dither-sequence sets of two to six photographs that belong together rather than random or mood sets, and keep the retro signal as atmosphere only without drawing monitor bezels or literal screen frames.
- Never show another business's identifiable space or product as the client's, avoid trade stock shots, use only library frames rated 7+ with no whole figures or mood-only content, and never display invented client logos.
- Build pages with six to nine sections including hero and footer (five on a thin prompt), deal at most two section moments per page each in its own section with at most one non-scroll moment in the hero or data-visual window, keep each section to one or two screens tall with nothing of the next section showing at load, and order sections by the argument the shape makes rather than a fixed pattern.
- Treat hover as a single contrast-raising signal: lift linkable cards 2px with a soft shadow shown only on hover, scale linked photographs 1.02–1.03 within their hidden-overflow frame without dimming or overlaying, and never animate focus rings.
- Keep every texture and surface static — no drifting grain, pulsing dots, or looping motion — and freeze ground/loop motion under reduced motion.
- Apply negative tracking to display faces (never letting two letters touch on condensed or heavy faces), apply text-wrap balance to headings and pretty to body copy, give uppercase wrapping hero headings a line-height of 1.0 or greater, and set manifesto headings as uppercase display type tilted between -2deg and -4deg or left straight using exactly one weight and one colour throughout.
- Maintain hierarchical h1-h6 headings progressing one level at a time, include an absolute-positioned skip-to-content link visible on focus as the first child of the header pointing to main content, and never use tabindex above 0.
- Never use tabs, sync stateful UI to the URL when state drives behavior, force each state in the preview with a helper class the component's CSS targets beside the real pseudo-class, and do not use CSS-only radio tabs at position:absolute;top:0 without a JS guard—put radios in flow at zero size or use preventScroll and use flex and grid for layout instead of JavaScript measurement.
- Judge object modules by count, ensuring more object modules are not loaded than canvases or fallback hosts drawn; keep at most four live WebGL contexts per page counting scenes with grounds and effects, and at most one per product page; mount an object or raymarched canvas when its panel nears the screen and destroy it when it leaves, keeping one per card group and rendering at most 1 CSS px per pixel, always showing a poster first and mounting each WebGL context at load or lazily as its section nears the screen.
- Ship every interactive component all eight states — default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, error, and success — with error applying the error colour to the border, message, and aria-invalid without relying on colour alone, and render focus as a 2px solid focus-colour ring with 2px offset shown only via :focus-visible.
- Do not use emoji, arrows, or non-checkable negation runs as list bullets, icon substitutes, or decorative elements in interface copy; use a single ellipsis character instead of three dots.
- Never use square brackets, lorem ipsum, to be added, TODO, kit sample text, empty hrefs, or credit and sample notes in the interface.
- Keep captions as factual text outside the image frame beside or under each picture, place credits in a footer data-credits line, and never claim a stand-in is the user's own person, place, product, or business.
- Set quantities in type rather than rendering 24 or more identical marks, except when marks form a chart differing in value or colour, and use at most one photograph of a collection with a small mark beside each entry.
- Maintain one light, one grade, and one register across all photographs, capture frames at 1440, 1024, 768, and 390 pixels and inspect every frame for empty or half-loaded areas, use a single border radius of 12 to 20px for software window photographs, and exclude light marks that vanish on light tiles.
- Use real icons in brand colours via local assets with data attributes at 20-40px, the real logo or file-type icon when one exists, and the UI icon set as inline SVGs in text colour, never calling a CDN.
- Compose the hero as the first screen (100svh) with the primary action alongside name, descriptor, and email, keeping the visual centre within 60px of the text block and no more than 120px from the viewport edge, and ensure nothing from the next section shows above the fold.
- The first screen must state what is sold, who it is for or where it applies, and include one hard fact, with the hero filling the screen alone at every width and its main button inside it; fill short heroes with only the visual or headline, never extra copy, and never leave more than 160px of empty space between the hero's lowest content and the fold or hero foot.
- Give stamped-position heroes full screen height and unstamped heroes less than 92dvh, keeping the headline, one sentence, and primary action visible at 1280x800 without scrolling, and never slice a custom wrapper at the fold.
- Allow at most one self-moving loop per container/panel, fit every diagram to one pulse cycle between 2.5 and 4 seconds, pause anything moving on its own for more than 5 seconds, and never start a loop on scroll.
- Ship the hero visual as the real thing with a small accent footprint under 2 MB, keep it present in markup for scripting-off and reduced-motion rendering, and never apply bounce, scrub-linked effects, or scroll-triggered reveals to the hero h1.
- Never move icons, never autoplay audio or video without user interaction, leave photographs outside linked cards with no hover effect, and make every photograph that leads somewhere a link that zooms 1.03 inside its frame while its card lifts 2px.
- Never auto-fire image generation; only render a product when the brief explicitly asks for 3D or a render and keep it as the product's own picture rather than an effect, never build a stat-led composition when the user has not provided a number, and never write a gate count you did not verify or claim a render pass unless Node 18+, puppeteer-core, and Chrome or Chromium are available.
- Sweep app screens with sloplint scope app (which skips the listed page-only gates and reads control heights on the app range), verify every effect in the gallery across light, dark, and colour palettes before shipping, ship only effects present in the current index and never reference retired effects, run measure when the curated list changes to update index.json boxes before fetching, and only assign a big scene to tech pages whose index.json type is software, tech, or gaming and never to places, services, or plain products.
- When the archive has written nothing yet, say so and wait without starting a build; if a referenced file fails to load, proceed on the Floor and state which file was unavailable; never claim a render pass when the render tier did not run and instead offer npm i -g puppeteer-core; exit immediately when no build is active except once for an unarmed tasteskill page changed in the last day; only close a build with --done when the user explicitly asks to stop or ship the page as is.
- Use at most three font families (at most four font files) with fixed jobs, prefer free variable fonts from Google Fonts or Fontshare, and only use paid foundries after the user confirms the licence; choose the pairing via the trade tool when available.
- Give every hover affordance a focus equivalent (never hover-only interactions), apply pointer-events:none and aria-hidden=true to texture overlays so they never intercept input, and measure text contrast against the textured result keeping it above thresholds.
- Use fx-prompt only for the hero prompt box of an AI or developer product, fx-bars only for monthly figures on a labelled scale with no more than sixteen bars, and a plain accent fade under a line or still ruled-grounds inside data, never an effect that draws its own grid or moving traces, never halftone-screen in a chart area, fx-tabs only for a product tour or dashboard preview, fx-avatar only for calls, presence, or a team list, and fx-headline only for hero or closing lines of two to three words.
- Ship no map when there is no build-time network or no street the brief gives, showing the visit's photograph instead, use fx-map only for visit or contact sections showing the real streets around the door, baked and still, use fx-icon-tiles only for a feature grid with drawn glyphs, never for brand logos, and use fx-spotlight only for a row of plain feature cards such as security or admin sections.
- Give every img data-source and data-subject attributes describing its origin and subject, wrap leading photographs in an <a class="m-zoom"> around its <img> or place img.frame__img inside a linked frame, and ensure page CSS clears a .frame[data-product]'s ground so it does not render on white.
- Never block paste via onPaste with preventDefault, apply overscroll-behavior: contain inside modals, drawers, and sheets, prefer native HTML elements over custom implementations when possible, and never set data-moments-over or data-moments-repeat on html.
- Build exactly one effect per page as either one live ground, one WebGL part, one object, or one big scene, never place it in a fixed layer, behind body copy, or as a page-long canvas, allow one card group to hold still grounds from the effect's family but never a second live effect, use only the system's, cards.css's, and the kit's classes and tokens, place any extra rule inside a visual or a place visit panel only, and never introduce a second grid, spacing scale, type scale, radius family, button shape, card recipe, hairline, corner-control size, link style or fade.
- Footer links must point only to existing pages and every link must land; forms must send, confirmations must appear only after a real send, each error must sit under its own field, and never emit success states for controls with no request behind them—keep an empty data-endpoint and proper empty states until the form is wired.
- Provide a real or absent play control, a stand-in poster that reads as the film's first frame, and no dead control bar or missing source; always show a poster before the live picture fades in, never an empty ground; hero images must use sizes="100vw", fetchpriority="high", and eager loading with a 4:5 phone crop in a picture element, never a vh term.
- Render steps as a real ordered list with numerals, never a 01 label over a head; every nav word, button, and footer link must point to an #id on the page, the logo to #top, the main action to where it is done, Contact to #contact, and Privacy/Imprint to written pages or leave them out; and only the middle plan may use .btn--primary.
- Use one primary button style, one container width, one column gap below the hero, one radius per nesting level, and at most one brand band and one lit border per page; keep accent ink where accent carries text, style pill controls with accent fill, and keep all chart labels/values/axis text in ink or muted with series identified by a swatch beside the label rather than coloured text.
- Allow at most two fixed, non-animated radial blooms sized 20–30% of the canvas and never a radial spotlight behind the hero; cap low-frequency fibre/paper-tone washes at 0.12 page-wide measured on the textured result, declare texture opacity via --texture-opacity (0.08 page-wide, 0.15 scoped to a single card or plate, 0.05 or none under running body text), and limit grid marks to one dark technical band, data panel, or footer never over text.
- Never ship a near-black page with a single neon accent, pure black/white paper or ink, or the most recognised generated looks on custom and unthemed builds unless explicitly requested, and treat framework defaults and gate-named looks as non-brand to restyle or replace.
- Never add captions, credits, or violet/lavender or blinking effects that explain or label a picture, and never state that an image is a render, drawing, or stand-in.
- State how to reach them with the form, street, and hours, keep roughly 80 words per screen, never ship placeholder or sample notes or printed example addresses, and never print an email or phone the brief does not give in the footer.
- Write every button and nav link as a verb plus its real object, keep helper lines to thirty characters or fewer without chevrons, and give every heading a noun naming a thing, section, or digit.
- Write foundry-voice copy naming the face, its weights, and its use in short phrases; keep tech copy to 60–80 words per screen outside UI mocks; name the game and genre in the h1/wordmark with launch date or status from the brief; and never invent a brand identity, uncommitted style, or logic.
- Name only customers the brief names, show whole-number counts with a change chip rather than percentages or carousels, show the main action at most twice per screen, and never present subscription plans for non-subscription offerings.
- A product with no 3D brief must never default to a 3D render; a CAD or render frame must sit on a light flat ground with square corners; never show a machine bearing its maker's name; and a game must lead with its own key art, in-engine shots, or trailer poster rather than library frames.
- Inspect every generated still and discard results with malformed anatomy, doubled furniture, or melted labels, and never leave a data-slot or gradient block larger than 300x200px showing only its wash—fill it with the user's image, a library photograph, or the fully built product at full fidelity.
- Keep total images under 2400 and the largest single image under 700 KiB.
- Use CSS Grid for pages and Flexbox inside components, shrinking flex children with min-width zero, giving image-bearing grid tracks minmax(0,1fr), and giving display heads overflow-wrap:break-word with min-width:0.
- Neighbouring sections must never share a layout or composition, and each section must use a different view.
- Build bento layouts with four to six tiles spanning six or twelve columns in mixed spans, structure content as card > panel > fragment without nesting patterned panels with white UI cards or cards inside cards, and use the specified tile classes for layout.
- Add a nav.actionbar fixed at the foot on places with one main action or two half-width buttons, showing only after the hero button scrolls off; on split navs place links in the left third, the logo on the center line, and actions in the right third, keeping four words that never wrap at 1024.
- Under reduced motion, resolve all motion instantly: collapse spatial motion to a 150ms opacity crossfade, keep functional state changes intact, show one still frame without pause controls, and stop decorative loops.
- Limit the page to one small entrance reveal, keep the hero fold static, and stagger below-the-fold content only; omit reveals on the first/index page.
- Restrict motion to transform, scale, opacity, and named cheap colour properties; never animate height, padding, layout, or move anything because the page scrolled.
- Never use carousels, self-advancing sliders, timed tabs, rotating notices, or spinners; pause any content moving longer than five seconds until scripts run, off-screen, or under reduced motion, and expose a visible pause control.
- Declare one motion character per system and scale durations accordingly, defaulting to still when motion adds nothing, keep at most one scroll-choreography pattern family per page, limit object motion to at most one per card group and two in view, and never move text at a speed different from the page.
- Carry at most one loop per card group, split, wide frame, or page via data-ui-loop with its pause button; never run two loops on one screen below the hero, and never move a place or feature card by itself.
- Identify the product, audience, and primary job from the brief or memory before designing, and confirm them with the client if the brief omits them.
