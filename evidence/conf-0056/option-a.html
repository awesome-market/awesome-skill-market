Field notes on the new accent

The surface uses a single near-black ink, never pure black, keeping everything neutral and calm.

Contrast check: body text on accent fill reads 4.6:1 — meets the 4.5:1 rule.

Each context emits its own paired ink token when text sits within 5% lightness of the fill.
