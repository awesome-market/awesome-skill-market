Field Notes on Quiet Design

Small observations about restraint, space, and the things we leave out.

Why Less Reads Better

When images dominate, text loses its voice. We keep photographs to a third of the page so writing stays the anchor.

Photographic shapes may reach up to 45 percent, but never two photo-led sections in a row.

The Rest of the Page

White space carries the structure. A single photograph per section keeps the rhythm calm and predictable.
