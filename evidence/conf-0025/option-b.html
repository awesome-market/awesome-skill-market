Stop feeding your week to the status meeting.

A small project-management tool for teams who value plain language over feature lists.

Three years ago I stopped trusting the word Sync. Every Monday it promised alignment and delivered three hours of my life back to nothing.

My team stopped sharing good news. They only reported blockers, quietly, into a thread that nobody read past the first line.

Then we tried a plain board. No dashboards. No “insights.” Just cards that moved when work moved.

Show the work. Skip the ceremony.

Six weeks later our stand-ups shrank to fifteen minutes and the blockers stopped multiplying. I finally saw the thing I had been missing: the work itself, unembellished.

Show the work. Skip the ceremony.

It is not magic. It is a tool that gets out of the way, the way good tools do when you are actually building something.

— Nadia, who still keeps one calendar block free every Monday morning.
